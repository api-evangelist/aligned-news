K-Dense BYOK is a free, MIT-licensed research assistant that runs on a scientist's own computer, records agent actions through an application-generated provenance log, and maintains a non-erasing Living Lab Notebook. Its linked evaluation compares the system with managed scientific platforms on 20 interdisciplinary prompts.
