Scientific Agents evaluates 100 profession profiles on roughly 4,500 questions from nine scientific benchmarks with matched models, user prompts and rule-based grading. The reported Gemini 3.8 Flash run loses 0.6 accuracy points overall and costs 2.2–4.5 times more per call.
