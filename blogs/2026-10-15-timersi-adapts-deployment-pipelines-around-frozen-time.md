Haoxin Liu, Deqing Fu and collaborators present TIMERSI, an agentic system that revises forecasting pipelines from historical errors while leaving the underlying foundation model unchanged. The arXiv preprint reports evaluation on 198 TIME and FEV-bench tasks.
