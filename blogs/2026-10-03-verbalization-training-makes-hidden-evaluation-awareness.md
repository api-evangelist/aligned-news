Usman Anwar, Sahar Abdelnabi and David Krueger train models to verbalize evaluation awareness more often without directly reinforcing the underlying belief. Across three model families, reported verbalization rates rise 2.4–2.9 times while measured latent awareness and task behavior remain largely stable.
